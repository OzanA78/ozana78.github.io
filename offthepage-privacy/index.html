Privacy Policy for Off The Page: Jump Escape

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Off The Page: Jump Escape ("we", "our", or "the app"), developed by OzDevAi, handles information when you use our application.

Information We Collect

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted by us:

  • Game settings (sound and music volume, tilt sensitivity, selected character)
  • The player name you enter (it does not need to be your real name)
  • Your local high score list
  • Which tutorial tips you have already seen

The app has no accounts and no online leaderboard.

Motion Sensor

The app reads your device's accelerometer to steer the character when you tilt your phone. Sensor readings are used only while you play and are not stored or transmitted.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to show optional rewarded ads (watched only if you choose to continue a game) and a banner ad on the game over screen. AdMob automatically collects and shares with its advertising partners data such as your device's advertising identifier, approximate location derived from your IP address, app interactions, and diagnostic information, in order to serve and measure ads. For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Analytics (Firebase Analytics)

We use Firebase Analytics (Google LLC) to understand how the app is used. It automatically collects app interaction data (such as app opens, screens viewed and session length), device identifiers (such as the app instance ID and advertising ID), and device information (such as device model, OS version and language).

Crash Reporting (Firebase Crashlytics)

We use Firebase Crashlytics (Google LLC) to detect and fix crashes. When the app crashes, it sends a crash log together with diagnostic information (such as device model, OS version and app version) and a Firebase installation identifier.

Remote Configuration (Firebase Remote Config) and App Updates

We use Firebase Remote Config to manage app settings remotely, such as the minimum required app version. If an update is needed, the app uses Google Play's in-app update service to offer it.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared with third parties only as described above: with Google LLC (AdMob, Firebase, Google Play) and its advertising partners for advertising, analytics, crash reporting and app updates.

Data Retention

  • Locally stored data remains on your device until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Analytics, crash and advertising data are retained by Google according to its own retention policies.

Data Deletion

We do not store any of your data on our own servers.

DataHow to delete it
Settings, player name, high scores and tips stored on your deviceUninstall the app or clear its data in your device settings
Analytics, crash and advertising data held by GoogleRetained and deleted by Google according to its own policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
